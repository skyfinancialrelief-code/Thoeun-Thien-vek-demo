# Mandatory Scientific Claim Boundaries

**Project:** VEK Assurance Cloud  
**Company:** GUTS Deterministic Technology LLC  

---

## Canonical Claim Statement

> “VEK Assurance Cloud does not make Gemini or any other probabilistic model deterministic and does not independently prove factual truth. It deterministically evaluates a captured model output against a disclosed demonstration policy. Given the same captured input, captured output, policy version, validator version, canonicalization profile, configuration, and initial state, the qualification process is designed to return the same decision and qualification hash.”

---

## Prohibited Claims Matrix

The application and all documentation MUST NOT claim that VEK:
1. Eliminates hallucinations.
2. Guarantees factual truth.
3. Guarantees operational or physical safety.
4. Makes Gemini or LLMs deterministic.
5. Is unhackable.
6. Is mathematically infallible.
7. Provides formal proof (unless machine-checked formal proofs exist).
8. Creates an immutable record solely by hashing data.
9. Provides FIPS validation.
10. Constitutes government certification.
11. Replaces qualified legal, financial, medical, cybersecurity, or engineering professionals.
